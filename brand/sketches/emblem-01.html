ATHENOA emblem, sketch 01

An oval flattened at top and bottom, one line around, a ribbon of lettering, and an inner field with a statue of Athena, rays of light coming from her head. The capitals are drawn by hand in one even stroke, like a cut inscription, so they match the line drawings and work in Greek.
